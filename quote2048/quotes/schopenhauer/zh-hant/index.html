    命運洗牌，我們出牌

    原文 · 德文Das Schicksal mischt die Karten, und wir spielen. (영역: “life is a game of cards, when the cards are shuffled and dealt by fate.”)

  2. 4

    人在小事上顯出性格

    — 叔本華

    原文 · 英文A man shows his character just in the way in which he deals with trifles,—for then he is off his guard.

  3. 8

    財富如海水，越喝越渴

    — 叔本華

    原文 · 英文Riches, one may say, are like sea-water; the more you drink the thirstier you become; and the same is true of fame.

  4. 16

    讀書是用別人的頭腦思考

    — 叔本華

    原文 · 德文Lesen heißt mit einem fremden Kopfe, statt des eigenen, denken. (영역: “Reading is thinking with some one else's head instead of one's own.”)

  5. 32

    文體是精神的相貌

    — 叔本華

    原文 · 德文Der Stil ist die Physiognomie des Geistes. (영역: “Style is the physiognomy of the mind, and a safer index to character than the face.”)

  6. 64

    人人把自己的眼界當作世界的邊界

    — 叔本華

    原文 · 英文Every man takes the limits of his own field of vision for the limits of the world.

  7. 128

    假設只吸收合乎自己的東西

    — 叔本華

    原文 · 英文When an hypothesis has once come to birth in the mind, or gained a footing there, it leads a life so far comparable with the life of an organism, as that it assimilates matter from the outer world only when it is like in kind with it and beneficial.

  8. 256

    唯有自制能擋住外來的強迫

    — 叔本華

    原文 · 英文Nothing will protect us from external compulsion so much as the control of ourselves.

  9. 512

    名聲要贏得，榮譽只需不失

    — 叔本華

    原文 · 德文Ruhm muß daher erst erworben werden: die Ehre hingegen braucht bloß nicht verloren zu gehen. (영역: “Fame is something which must be won; honor, only something which must not be lost.”)

  10. 1024

    人愛合群，因為忍不了孤獨

    — 叔本華

    原文 · 德文Was nun andrerseits die Menschen gesellig macht, ist ihre Unfähigkeit, die Einsamkeit und in dieser sich selbst zu ertragen.
