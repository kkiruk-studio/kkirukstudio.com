HOW IT WORKS01–03

Scan, swipe, keep it in Photos.

01SCAN

Scan your camera roll

CapBox counts the captures you haven't sorted yet. Work through screenshots only, or all your photos.

02SORT

Swipe and tap a folder

Swipe through one at a time and tap a folder chip. Or select a batch and send it all at once. Anything you don't need goes to the trash.

03KEEP

Stays as real Photos albums

Folders are real albums inside a “CapBox” folder in the Photos app, so your sorting stays even if you delete CapBox.

CORE VALUEON-DEVICE

Sorted, but never uploaded.

Text recognition and search both run on your iPhone. There's no server, so there's nowhere to send your photos. No account, no in-app purchases.

CapBox · About this app

CapBox is an iPhone app that sorts screenshots into folders one swipe at a time, clears the ones you do not need to the trash, and searches the text inside your photos. Everything runs on-device, it is free and has no in-app purchases.
