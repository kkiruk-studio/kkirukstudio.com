About this palette

Christina's World is a painting by Andrew Wyeth, dated 1948. It comes from North American art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from brown (#6F5E3A) up to silver gray (#E1D6D3), passing through olive, dusty rose, khaki. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 41 to 86 in CIE Lab).
