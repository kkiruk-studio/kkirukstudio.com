About this palette

The Kiss is a painting by Gustav Klimt, dated 1908. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from olive (#896D23) up to sand (#CFBB82), passing through terracotta, violet, gold, mustard. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 47 to 76 in CIE Lab).
