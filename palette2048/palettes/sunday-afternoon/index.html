About this palette

A Sunday Afternoon is a painting by Georges Seurat, dated 1886. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark green (#24362D) up to sand (#AA9D86), passing through navy, dusty blue, olive. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 21 to 65 in CIE Lab).
