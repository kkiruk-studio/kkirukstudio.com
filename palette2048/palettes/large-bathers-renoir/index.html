About this palette

The Large Bathers is a painting by Pierre-Auguste Renoir, dated 1887. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from brown (#64321E) up to beige (#CDCFBE), passing through sienna, moss green, lavender, peach. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 27 to 83 in CIE Lab).
