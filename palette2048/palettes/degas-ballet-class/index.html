About this palette

The Ballet Class is a painting by Edgar Degas, dated 1874. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark green (#2A462D) up to silver gray (#D6BEC5), passing through forest green, brown, olive, khaki. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 27 to 79 in CIE Lab).
