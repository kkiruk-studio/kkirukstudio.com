About this palette

Wanderer Above the Sea of Fog is a painting by Caspar David Friedrich, dated 1818. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#100C15) up to silver gray (#CACBDD), passing through blue gray, gray, khaki, sky blue. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, mostly cool (lightness 4 to 82 in CIE Lab).
