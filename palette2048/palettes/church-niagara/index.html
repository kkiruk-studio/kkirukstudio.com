About this palette

Niagara is a painting by Frederic Edwin Church, dated 1857. It comes from North American art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark green (#272C1A) up to ivory (#EEF2E5), passing through forest green, brown, sienna, sky blue. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 17 to 95 in CIE Lab).
