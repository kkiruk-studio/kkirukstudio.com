About this palette

The Scream is a painting by Edvard Munch, dated 1893. It comes from Northern European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#2D2623) up to mustard (#DEAC5B), passing through deep blue, teal, sienna, khaki. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 16 to 73 in CIE Lab).
