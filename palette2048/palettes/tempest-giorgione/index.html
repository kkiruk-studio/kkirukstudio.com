About this palette

The Tempest is a painting by Giorgione, dated 1508. It comes from Western European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from dark green (#283615) up to sand (#C1AF99), passing through brown, teal, olive, sage. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 21 to 72 in CIE Lab).
