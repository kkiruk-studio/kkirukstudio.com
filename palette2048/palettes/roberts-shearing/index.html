About this palette

Shearing the Rams is a painting by Tom Roberts, dated 1890. It comes from Oceanian art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from maroon (#411007) up to ochre (#CF8A29), passing through rust, sienna, ochre. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 13 to 63 in CIE Lab).
