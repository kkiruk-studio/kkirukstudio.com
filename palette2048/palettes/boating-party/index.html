About this palette

Luncheon of the Boating Party is a painting by Pierre-Auguste Renoir, dated 1881. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#18272D) up to beige (#E8DFC6), passing through dark green, rust, terracotta, ochre. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 15 to 89 in CIE Lab).
