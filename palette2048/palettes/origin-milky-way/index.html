About this palette

The Origin of the Milky Way is a painting by Tintoretto, dated 1575. It comes from Western European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#292420) up to olive (#987038), passing through dark umber, charcoal, brown. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 15 to 50 in CIE Lab).
