About this palette

España y Filipinas is a painting by Juan Luna, dated 1886. It comes from Southeast Asian art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#23191E) up to beige (#F0D7B9), passing through plum, slate gray, terracotta, salmon. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 10 to 87 in CIE Lab).
