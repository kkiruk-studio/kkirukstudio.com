About this palette

Mountains at Collioure is a painting by André Derain, dated 1905. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1E281E) up to pale yellow (#EBDC96), passing through crimson, steel blue, sea green, orange. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 15 to 88 in CIE Lab).
