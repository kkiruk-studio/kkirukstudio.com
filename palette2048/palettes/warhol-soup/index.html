About this palette

Campbell's Soup Cans is a painting by Andy Warhol, dated 1962. It comes from North American art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from black (#17110F) up to off-white (#F1EFE6), passing through red, gray, khaki, silver gray. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 6 to 94 in CIE Lab).
