About this palette

Baba Yaga is a painting by Ivan Bilibin, dated 1900. It comes from Eastern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#161E16) up to cream (#E1D7AA), passing through plum, forest green, burnt orange, khaki. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 10 to 86 in CIE Lab).
