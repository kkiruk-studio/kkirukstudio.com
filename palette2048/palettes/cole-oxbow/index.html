About this palette

The Oxbow is a painting by Thomas Cole, dated 1836. It comes from North American art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from dark green (#2B3915) up to silver gray (#D3C4D8), passing through olive, brown, gold, skin tone. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 22 to 81 in CIE Lab).
