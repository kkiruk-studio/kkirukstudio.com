About this palette

Game of Riding Hawk is a painting by Shin Yun-bok, dated 1810. It comes from Korean art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#072A32) up to silver gray (#DBD7E7), passing through salmon, pale yellow, lavender. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 15 to 87 in CIE Lab).
