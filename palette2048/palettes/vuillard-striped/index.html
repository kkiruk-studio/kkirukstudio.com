About this palette

Woman in a Striped Dress is a painting by Édouard Vuillard, dated 1895. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1C231E) up to beige (#E1D7B4), passing through rust, moss green, blue gray, khaki. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 13 to 86 in CIE Lab).
