About this palette

At the Moulin Rouge is a painting by Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec, dated 1895. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#2B2419) up to pale yellow (#E7D694), passing through rust, sea green, gold. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 15 to 86 in CIE Lab).
