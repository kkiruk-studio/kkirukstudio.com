About this palette

Portrait of a Lady is a painting by Aina Onabolu, dated 1921. It comes from African art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#393515) up to mustard (#CBA33B), passing through brown, crimson, red, gold. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 22 to 69 in CIE Lab).
