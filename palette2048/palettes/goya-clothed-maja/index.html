About this palette

La Maja Vestida is a painting by Francisco Goya, dated 1805. It comes from Southern European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#2C2015) up to off-white (#E5E5E2), passing through charcoal, olive, salmon, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 13 to 91 in CIE Lab).
