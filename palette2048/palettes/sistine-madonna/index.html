About this palette

Sistine Madonna is a painting by Raphael, dated 1512. It comes from Southern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#251810) up to ochre (#C8822C), passing through dark umber, charcoal, sienna. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 10 to 60 in CIE Lab).
