About this palette

Nude Descending a Staircase is a painting by Marcel Duchamp, dated 1912. It comes from Northern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#272724) up to khaki (#C5B373), passing through dark umber, olive. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 16 to 73 in CIE Lab).
