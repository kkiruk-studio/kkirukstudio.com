About this palette

The Cyclist is a painting by Natalia Goncharova, dated 1913. It comes from Eastern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#121110) up to beige (#E6DAC4), passing through cobalt blue, burnt orange, khaki. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 5 to 87 in CIE Lab).
