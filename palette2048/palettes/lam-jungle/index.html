About this palette

The Jungle is a painting by Wifredo Lam, dated 1943. It comes from Latin American art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#081D2A) up to cream (#FFEFD1), passing through dark green, red, ochre, yellow. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 10 to 95 in CIE Lab).
