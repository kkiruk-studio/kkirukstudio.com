About this palette

The Painter is a painting by Marlene Dumas, dated 1994. It comes from African art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from black (#1B1B21) up to ivory (#FFEED8), passing through cobalt blue, red, khaki, skin tone. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 10 to 95 in CIE Lab).
