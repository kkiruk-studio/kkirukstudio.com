About this palette

The Charging Chasseur is a painting by Théodore Géricault, dated 1812. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#161411) up to ivory (#E6E1D7), passing through dark green, rust, ochre, khaki. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 6 to 90 in CIE Lab).
