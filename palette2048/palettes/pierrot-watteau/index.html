About this palette

Pierrot is a painting by Jean-Antoine Watteau, dated 1719. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#2B221D) up to silver gray (#C6B8AB), passing through rust, sienna, gray, dusty blue. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 14 to 76 in CIE Lab).
