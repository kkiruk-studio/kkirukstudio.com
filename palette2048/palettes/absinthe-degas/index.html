About this palette

L’Absinthe is a painting by Edgar Degas, dated 1876. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1B1215) up to khaki (#B2A26C), passing through dark umber. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 7 to 67 in CIE Lab).
