About this palette

Man on a Balcony, Boulevard Haussmann is a painting by Gustave Caillebotte, dated 1880. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#161B2A) up to cream (#E8DBB1), passing through dark umber, brown, olive, sky blue. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 10 to 87 in CIE Lab).
