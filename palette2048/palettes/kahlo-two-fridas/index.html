About this palette

The Two Fridas is a painting by Frida Kahlo, dated 1939. It comes from Latin American art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#191E28) up to off-white (#EBE6E1), passing through crimson, red, teal, slate gray. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 11 to 92 in CIE Lab).
