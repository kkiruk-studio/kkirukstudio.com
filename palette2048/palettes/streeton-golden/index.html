About this palette

Golden Summer, Eaglemont is a painting by Arthur Streeton, dated 1889. It comes from Oceanian art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark green (#283228) up to cream (#EEE6C3), passing through steel blue, moss green, ochre, pale yellow. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 20 to 91 in CIE Lab).
