About this palette

The Great Wave is a painting by Katsushika Hokusai, dated 1831. It comes from Japanese art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from navy (#181A3C) up to off-white (#F8F7EE), passing through deep blue, skin tone, beige. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 11 to 97 in CIE Lab).
