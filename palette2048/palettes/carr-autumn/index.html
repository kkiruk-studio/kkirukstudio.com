About this palette

Autumn in France is a painting by Emily Carr, dated 1911. It comes from North American art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1E231E) up to pale yellow (#EBDCA0), passing through steel blue, sienna, moss green, ochre. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 13 to 88 in CIE Lab).
