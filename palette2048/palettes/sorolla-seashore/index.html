About this palette

Strolling along the Seashore is a painting by Joaquín Sorolla, dated 1909. It comes from Southern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#282D46) up to off-white (#F0EBE1), passing through dusty blue, lavender, tan, beige. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 19 to 93 in CIE Lab).
