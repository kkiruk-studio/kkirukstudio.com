About this palette

Sleeping Venus is a painting by Giorgione, dated 1510. It comes from Western European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#1E1812) up to ivory (#F4EEDC), passing through rust, olive, sky blue, beige. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 9 to 94 in CIE Lab).
