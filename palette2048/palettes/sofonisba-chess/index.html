About this palette

The Chess Game is a painting by Sofonisba Anguissola, dated 1555. It comes from Southern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#14100C) up to ivory (#DADECD), passing through dark green, burnt orange, skin tone, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 5 to 88 in CIE Lab).
