About this palette

Impression, Sunrise is a painting by Claude Monet, dated 1872. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#254D55) up to skin tone (#C8997B), passing through dark green, blue gray, slate gray, burnt orange. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 30 to 67 in CIE Lab).
