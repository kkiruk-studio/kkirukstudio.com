About this palette

Court of Gayumars is a painting by Sultan Muhammad, dated 1525. It comes from Middle Eastern art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from navy (#202438) up to ivory (#ECE2CE), passing through charcoal, terracotta, salmon, khaki. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 15 to 90 in CIE Lab).
