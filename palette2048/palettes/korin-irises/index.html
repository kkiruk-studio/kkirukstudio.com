About this palette

Irises is a painting by Ogata Kōrin, dated c. 1701. It comes from Japanese art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from deep blue (#2F3479) up to pale yellow (#EFD680), passing through brown, sea green, mustard. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 25 to 86 in CIE Lab).
