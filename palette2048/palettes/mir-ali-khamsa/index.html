About this palette

Khamsa Illustrations is a painting by Mir Sayyid Ali, dated 1540. It comes from Middle Eastern art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from brown (#6D4100) up to ivory (#F2E6D0), passing through steel blue, sienna, gray, mustard. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 32 to 92 in CIE Lab).
