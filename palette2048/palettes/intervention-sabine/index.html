About this palette

The Intervention of the Sabine Women is a painting by Jacques-Louis David, dated 1799. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#392B24) up to silver gray (#99A7B3), passing through sienna, dusty rose, gray, slate gray. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 19 to 68 in CIE Lab).
