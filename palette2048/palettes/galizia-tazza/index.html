About this palette

Glass Tazza with Peaches is a painting by Fede Galizia, dated 1607. It comes from Southern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#1C1816) up to cream (#EBE1C3), passing through gray, sienna, moss green, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 9 to 90 in CIE Lab).
