About this palette

Tiger and Magpies is a painting by Korean Folk Painting, dated 19th c.. It comes from Korean art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#14120F) up to ivory (#F0E6D2), passing through dark green, brown, sienna, ochre. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 6 to 92 in CIE Lab).
