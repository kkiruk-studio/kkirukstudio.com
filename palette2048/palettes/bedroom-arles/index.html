About this palette

Bedroom in Arles is a painting by Vincent van Gogh, dated 1888. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#293E57) up to cream (#F0E6C8), passing through forest green, dusty blue, moss green, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 26 to 91 in CIE Lab).
