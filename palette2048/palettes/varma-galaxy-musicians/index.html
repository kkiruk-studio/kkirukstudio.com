About this palette

Galaxy of Musicians is a painting by Raja Ravi Varma, dated 1889. It comes from South Asian art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1E1419) up to sand (#EBD2A0), passing through ultramarine, teal, crimson, gold. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 8 to 85 in CIE Lab).
