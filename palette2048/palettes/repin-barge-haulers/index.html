About this palette

Barge Haulers on the Volga is a painting by Ilya Repin, dated 1873. It comes from Eastern European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from orange (#DB9212) up to beige (#F6D6BC), passing through mustard, lavender. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 66 to 88 in CIE Lab).
