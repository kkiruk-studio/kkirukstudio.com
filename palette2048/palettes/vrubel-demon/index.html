About this palette

The Demon Seated is a painting by Mikhail Vrubel, dated 1890. It comes from Eastern European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from maroon (#3A1722) up to mustard (#F2B661), passing through dark green, deep blue, rust, khaki. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 13 to 78 in CIE Lab).
