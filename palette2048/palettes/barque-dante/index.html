About this palette

The Barque of Dante is a painting by Eugène Delacroix, dated 1822. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#161812) up to tan (#E1C3A5), passing through navy, crimson, sienna, olive. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 8 to 81 in CIE Lab).
