About this palette

Marilyn Diptych is a painting by Andy Warhol, dated 1962. It comes from North American art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from navy (#0F1D2F) up to powder blue (#D8E4F4), passing through turquoise, lavender, pale yellow. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 10 to 90 in CIE Lab).
