About this palette

The Night Watch is a painting by Rembrandt van Rijn, dated 1642. It comes from Northern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#0D0B08) up to sand (#D6BB83), passing through dark umber, olive. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 3 to 77 in CIE Lab).
