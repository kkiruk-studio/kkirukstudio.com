About this palette

The Coronation of Napoleon is a painting by Jacques-Louis David, dated 1807. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#16170F) up to beige (#DDCDAF), passing through dark umber, brown, rust, ochre. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 7 to 83 in CIE Lab).
