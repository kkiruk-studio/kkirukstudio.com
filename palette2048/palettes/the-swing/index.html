About this palette

The Swing is a painting by Jean-Honoré Fragonard, dated 1767. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from dark green (#1F3724) up to cream (#F4E0BF), passing through forest green, moss green, dusty rose. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 21 to 90 in CIE Lab).
