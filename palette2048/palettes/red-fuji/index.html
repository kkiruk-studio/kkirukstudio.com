About this palette

Red Fuji is a painting by Katsushika Hokusai, dated 1831. It comes from Japanese art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#244359) up to cream (#DFD6AD), passing through teal, terracotta, salmon, sky blue. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 27 to 85 in CIE Lab).
