About this palette

Washington Crossing the Delaware is a painting by Emanuel Leutze, dated 1851. It comes from North American art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1F110D) up to silver gray (#CAC5DC), passing through dark umber, rust, steel blue, skin tone. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 7 to 81 in CIE Lab).
