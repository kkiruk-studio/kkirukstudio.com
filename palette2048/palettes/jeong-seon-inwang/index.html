About this palette

Inwangjesaekdo is a painting by Jeong Seon, dated 1751. It comes from Korean art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#33333D) up to off-white (#DCDFDC), passing through slate gray, silver gray, sage. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 22 to 89 in CIE Lab).
