About this palette

Fine Wind, Clear Morning is a painting by Katsushika Hokusai, dated c. 1830. It comes from Japanese art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from maroon (#3C1817) up to ivory (#DBDBD1), passing through navy, terracotta, dusty rose, silver gray. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 14 to 87 in CIE Lab).
