About this palette

Grande Odalisque is a painting by Jean-Auguste-Dominique Ingres, dated 1814. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#232734) up to beige (#CFC1AB), passing through deep blue, ultramarine, skin tone, gold. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 16 to 79 in CIE Lab).
