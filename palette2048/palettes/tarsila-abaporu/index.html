About this palette

Abaporu is a painting by Tarsila do Amaral, dated 1928. It comes from Latin American art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from dark green (#14281E) up to yellow (#F0D246), passing through steel blue, sea green, sky blue, skin tone. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 14 to 85 in CIE Lab).
