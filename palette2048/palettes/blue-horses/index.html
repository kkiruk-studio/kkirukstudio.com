About this palette

Large Blue Horses is a painting by Franz Marc, dated 1911. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#05132F) up to red (#C01F10), passing through midnight blue, cobalt blue. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly cool (lightness 6 to 42 in CIE Lab).
