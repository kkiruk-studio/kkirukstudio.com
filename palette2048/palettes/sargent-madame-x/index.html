About this palette

Madame X is a painting by John Singer Sargent, dated 1884. It comes from North American art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#0D121B) up to peach (#E6C4AC), passing through dark umber, brown, tan. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 5 to 81 in CIE Lab).
