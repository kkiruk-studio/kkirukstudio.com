About this palette

Paris Street, Rainy Day is a painting by Gustave Caillebotte, dated 1877. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#1C293D) up to beige (#DAD5C1), passing through dark umber, dark green, rust, powder blue. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 16 to 85 in CIE Lab).
