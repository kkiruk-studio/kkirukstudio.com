About this palette

American Gothic is a painting by Grant Wood, dated 1930. It comes from North American art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1D2422) up to cream (#C4DFA9), passing through dark umber, moss green, khaki. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 13 to 86 in CIE Lab).
