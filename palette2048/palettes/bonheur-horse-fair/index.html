About this palette

The Horse Fair is a painting by Rosa Bonheur, dated 1855. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark green (#272C19) up to cream (#D2E1BE), passing through dark umber, olive, gray. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 17 to 88 in CIE Lab).
