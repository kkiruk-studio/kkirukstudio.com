About this palette

Autumn Colors on the Que and Hua Mountains is a painting by Zhao Mengfu, dated 1296. It comes from Chinese art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from khaki (#987950) up to sand (#DEC7A2), passing through terracotta, tan. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 53 to 81 in CIE Lab).
