About this palette

Early Spring is a painting by Guo Xi, dated 1072. It comes from Chinese art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from black (#111804) up to orange (#F2761A), passing through olive, ochre. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 7 to 63 in CIE Lab).
