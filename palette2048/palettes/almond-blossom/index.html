About this palette

Almond Blossom is a painting by Vincent van Gogh, dated 1890. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from moss green (#628A50) up to pale yellow (#C6D395), passing through dusty blue, sage, turquoise, powder blue. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 53 to 82 in CIE Lab).
