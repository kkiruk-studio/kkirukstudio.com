About this palette

The Wounded Deer is a painting by Frida Kahlo, dated 1946. It comes from Latin American art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from black (#211813) up to ivory (#F1ECD5), passing through brown, red, sienna, powder blue. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 9 to 93 in CIE Lab).
