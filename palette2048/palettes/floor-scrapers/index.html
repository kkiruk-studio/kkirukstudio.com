About this palette

The Floor Scrapers is a painting by Gustave Caillebotte, dated 1875. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#2B221C) up to ivory (#ECE2CC), passing through charcoal, brown, ochre, tan. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 14 to 90 in CIE Lab).
