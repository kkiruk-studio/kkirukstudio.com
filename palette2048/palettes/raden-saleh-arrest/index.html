About this palette

The Arrest of Pangeran Diponegoro is a painting by Raden Saleh, dated 1857. It comes from Southeast Asian art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#221812) up to beige (#E2D8C0), passing through dark green, charcoal, rust, dusty blue. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 9 to 87 in CIE Lab).
