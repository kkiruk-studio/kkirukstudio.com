About this palette

L'étoile (The Star) is a painting by Edgar Degas, dated 1878. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from brown (#584919) up to powder blue (#81C9C9), passing through blue gray, moss green, ochre, slate gray. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 32 to 76 in CIE Lab).
