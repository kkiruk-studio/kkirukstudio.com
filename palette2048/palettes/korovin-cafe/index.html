About this palette

Paris, Café de la Paix is a painting by Konstantin Korovin, dated 1906. It comes from Eastern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#141928) up to pale yellow (#EBD7A0), passing through blue gray, terracotta, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 9 to 86 in CIE Lab).
