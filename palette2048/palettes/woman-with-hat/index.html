About this palette

Woman with a Hat is a painting by Henri Matisse, dated 1905. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from ultramarine (#48428A) up to tan (#D7B78F), passing through sienna, ochre, dusty rose. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 32 to 76 in CIE Lab).
