About this palette

Bani Thani is a painting by Nihal Chand, dated 1750. It comes from South Asian art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#192319) up to pale yellow (#EBD7A0), passing through violet, sea green, coral, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 12 to 86 in CIE Lab).
