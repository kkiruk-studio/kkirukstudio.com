About this palette

A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains is a painting by Wang Ximeng, dated 1113. It comes from Chinese art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from olive (#6C5D29) up to pale yellow (#ECD39C), passing through mustard. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 40 to 85 in CIE Lab).
