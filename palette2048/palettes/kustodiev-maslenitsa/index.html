About this palette

Maslenitsa (Shrovetide) is a painting by Boris Kustodiev, dated 1919. It comes from Eastern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#1E2337) up to off-white (#EBEBEB), passing through crimson, dusty blue, pink, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 14 to 93 in CIE Lab).
