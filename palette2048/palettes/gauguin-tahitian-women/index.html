About this palette

Tahitian Women on the Beach is a painting by Paul Gauguin, dated 1891. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#141A22) up to cream (#F0E2C2), passing through dark green, red, ochre, pink. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 9 to 90 in CIE Lab).
