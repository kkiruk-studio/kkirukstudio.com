About this palette

The Ray is a painting by Jean-Baptiste-Siméon Chardin, dated 1728. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#120D04) up to sienna (#AC6831), passing through dark umber, dark green, brown. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 4 to 51 in CIE Lab).
