About this palette

Jane Avril is a painting by Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec, dated 1892. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark green (#2A3423) up to beige (#E8E2CA), passing through sienna, moss green, sand. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 20 to 90 in CIE Lab).
