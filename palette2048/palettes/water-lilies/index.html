About this palette

Water Lilies is a painting by Claude Monet, dated 1906. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#363E58) up to mustard (#D8C75C), passing through teal, moss green, lavender. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 27 to 80 in CIE Lab).
