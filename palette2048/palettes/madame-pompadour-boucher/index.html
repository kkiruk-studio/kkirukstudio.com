About this palette

Madame de Pompadour is a painting by François Boucher, dated 1756. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#150E05) up to ivory (#EAE1D0), passing through olive, khaki, skin tone, sand. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 4 to 90 in CIE Lab).
