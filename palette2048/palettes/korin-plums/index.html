About this palette

Red and White Plum Blossoms is a painting by Ogata Kōrin, dated c. 1710s. It comes from Japanese art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from maroon (#320D10) up to pale yellow (#FCE68F), passing through plum, orange, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 9 to 91 in CIE Lab).
