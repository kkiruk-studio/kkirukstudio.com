About this palette

White Ox is a painting by Lee Jung-seob, dated 1954. It comes from Korean art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#314547) up to ivory (#F6EFD5), passing through gray, khaki, tan. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 28 to 94 in CIE Lab).
