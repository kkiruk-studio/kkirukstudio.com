About this palette

Las Meninas is a painting by Diego Velázquez, dated 1656. It comes from Southern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#100D06) up to sand (#D8C19A), passing through dark umber, olive, terracotta. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 4 to 79 in CIE Lab).
