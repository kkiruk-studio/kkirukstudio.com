About this palette

Carnation, Lily, Lily, Rose is a painting by John Singer Sargent, dated 1885-1886. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#5B3630) up to ivory (#ECE7CF), passing through dark green, dusty blue, dusty rose, mustard. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 27 to 91 in CIE Lab).
