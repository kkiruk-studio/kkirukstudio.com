About this palette

A Bar at the Folies-Bergère is a painting by Édouard Manet, dated 1882. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#16202E) up to sage (#98AD98), passing through charcoal, burnt orange. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, mostly cool (lightness 12 to 69 in CIE Lab).
