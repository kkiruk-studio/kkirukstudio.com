About this palette

Garden at Sainte-Adresse is a painting by Claude Monet, dated 1867. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#163246) up to ivory (#F5F0E1), passing through blue gray, red, dusty blue, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 20 to 95 in CIE Lab).
