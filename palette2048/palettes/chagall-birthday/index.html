About this palette

The Birthday is a painting by Marc Chagall, dated 1915. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from rust (#7B1A1B) up to beige (#CDC7B5), passing through steel blue, sand. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 27 to 80 in CIE Lab).
