About this palette

The City is a painting by Fernand Léger, dated 1919. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#19191C) up to ivory (#EBE1D2), passing through ultramarine, vermilion, slate gray, yellow. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 9 to 90 in CIE Lab).
