About this palette

Open Window, Collioure is a painting by Henri Matisse, dated 1905. It comes from French art of the modern era.

Its six colors run from black (#0C212A) up to beige (#ECC8C4), passing through charcoal, ultramarine, red, burnt orange. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 12 to 84 in CIE Lab).
