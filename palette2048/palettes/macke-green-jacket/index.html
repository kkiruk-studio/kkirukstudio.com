About this palette

Lady in a Green Jacket is a painting by August Macke, dated 1913. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#3D2E2B) up to pale yellow (#DED096), passing through blue gray, brown, forest green, sienna. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 21 to 83 in CIE Lab).
