About this palette

Beata Beatrix is a painting by Dante Gabriel Rossetti, dated 1870. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1A1612) up to mustard (#BA9638), passing through dark umber. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 8 to 64 in CIE Lab).
