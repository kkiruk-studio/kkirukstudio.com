About this palette

The Umbrellas is a painting by Pierre-Auguste Renoir, dated 1886. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#233344) up to khaki (#B79165), passing through blue gray, olive, brown. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 21 to 63 in CIE Lab).
