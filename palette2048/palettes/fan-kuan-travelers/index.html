About this palette

Travelers Among Mountains and Streams is a painting by Fan Kuan, dated 1000. It comes from Chinese art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from black (#140F0A) up to tan (#E6C88C), passing through dark green, brown, khaki. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 5 to 82 in CIE Lab).
