About this palette

Danaë is a painting by Titian, dated 1544. It comes from Western European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#16120F) up to beige (#EBDEC3), passing through rust, steel blue, dusty rose, skin tone. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 6 to 89 in CIE Lab).
