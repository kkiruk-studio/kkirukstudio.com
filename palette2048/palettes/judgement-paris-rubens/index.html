About this palette

The Judgement of Paris is a painting by Peter Paul Rubens, dated 1636. It comes from Western European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#231B13) up to khaki (#AA8644), passing through dark umber, brown, olive. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 10 to 58 in CIE Lab).
