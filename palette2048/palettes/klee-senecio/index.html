About this palette

Senecio is a painting by Paul Klee, dated 1922. It comes from Northern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from plum (#713665) up to tan (#D6B98F), passing through burnt orange, magenta, ochre, mustard. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 32 to 77 in CIE Lab).
