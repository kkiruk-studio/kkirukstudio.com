About this palette

Café Terrace at Night is a painting by Vincent van Gogh, dated 1888. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#18111A) up to mustard (#EBC03C), passing through deep blue, cobalt blue, olive, burnt orange. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 6 to 79 in CIE Lab).
