About this palette

Mona Lisa is a painting by Leonardo da Vinci, dated 1503. It comes from Southern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from midnight blue (#190E2B) up to moss green (#B3B750), passing through maroon, sienna, skin tone. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 6 to 72 in CIE Lab).
