About this palette

The Apotheosis of Homer is a painting by Jean-Auguste-Dominique Ingres, dated 1827. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from navy (#1B234B) up to beige (#E5DAB8), passing through steel blue, burnt orange, teal, tan. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 15 to 87 in CIE Lab).
