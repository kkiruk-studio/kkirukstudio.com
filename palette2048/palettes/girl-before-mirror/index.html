About this palette

Girl Before a Mirror is a painting by Pablo Picasso, dated 1932. It comes from Southern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#240C07) up to peach (#F9D2A5), passing through dark umber, brown, ochre. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 6 to 86 in CIE Lab).
