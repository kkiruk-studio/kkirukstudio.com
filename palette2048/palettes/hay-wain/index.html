About this palette

The Hay Wain is a painting by John Constable, dated 1821. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#180F16) up to silver gray (#C8D5DA), passing through dark umber, olive, moss green, steel blue. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 5 to 84 in CIE Lab).
