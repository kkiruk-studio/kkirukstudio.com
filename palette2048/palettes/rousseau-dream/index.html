About this palette

The Dream is a painting by Henri Rousseau, dated 1910. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from dark green (#0C2A20) up to ivory (#EBE8DC), passing through forest green, pink, sky blue, peach. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 15 to 92 in CIE Lab).
