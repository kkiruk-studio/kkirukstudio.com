About this palette

The Boating Party is a painting by Mary Cassatt, dated 1894. It comes from North American art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#102636) up to ivory (#EBE6D7), passing through red, moss green, skin tone, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 14 to 91 in CIE Lab).
