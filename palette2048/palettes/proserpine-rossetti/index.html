About this palette

Proserpine is a painting by Dante Gabriel Rossetti, dated 1874. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1C1E17) up to khaki (#9B7C51), passing through dark umber, dark green. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 11 to 54 in CIE Lab).
