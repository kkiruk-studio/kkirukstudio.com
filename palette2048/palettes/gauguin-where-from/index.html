About this palette

Where Do We Come From? is a painting by Paul Gauguin, dated 1898. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#0E1C2A) up to beige (#DECEB4), passing through dark green, dusty blue, ochre, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 10 to 83 in CIE Lab).
