About this palette

Ariadne Abandoned by Theseus is a painting by Angelica Kauffman, dated 1774. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#1E1E28) up to beige (#E6DCC3), passing through crimson, steel blue, gray, skin tone. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 12 to 88 in CIE Lab).
