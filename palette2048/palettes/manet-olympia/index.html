About this palette

Olympia is a painting by Édouard Manet, dated 1863. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#161919) up to beige (#DDDDC1), passing through dark umber, terracotta, sage, tan. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 8 to 87 in CIE Lab).
