About this palette

One Hundred Famous Views of Edo is a painting by Utagawa Hiroshige, dated 1856. It comes from Japanese art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#2A3244) up to off-white (#F6F6EE), passing through teal, red, skin tone, beige. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 21 to 97 in CIE Lab).
