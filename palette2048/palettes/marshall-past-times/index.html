About this palette

Past Times is a painting by Kerry James Marshall, dated 1997. It comes from North American art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from black (#1B1B1B) up to off-white (#F3F1E6), passing through crimson, ultramarine, emerald, yellow. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 10 to 95 in CIE Lab).
