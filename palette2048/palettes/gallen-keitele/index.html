About this palette

Lake Keitele is a painting by Akseli Gallen-Kallela, dated 1905. It comes from Northern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#192837) up to off-white (#DEE4E8), passing through charcoal, blue gray, dusty blue, powder blue. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, mostly cool (lightness 15 to 90 in CIE Lab).
