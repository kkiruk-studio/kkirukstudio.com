About this palette

Napoleon Crossing the Alps is a painting by Jacques-Louis David, dated 1801. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#303940) up to beige (#E4DECA), passing through red, brown, mustard, silver gray. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 23 to 88 in CIE Lab).
