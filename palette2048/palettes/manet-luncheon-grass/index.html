About this palette

The Luncheon on the Grass is a painting by Édouard Manet, dated 1863. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#161A10) up to ivory (#EBE6D2), passing through dark green, navy, olive, beige. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 9 to 91 in CIE Lab).
