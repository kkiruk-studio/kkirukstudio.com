About this palette

The Cradle is a painting by Berthe Morisot, dated 1872. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#261400) up to pale yellow (#FFE6AF), passing through mustard, khaki, tan. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 8 to 92 in CIE Lab).
