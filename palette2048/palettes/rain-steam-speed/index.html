About this palette

Rain, Steam and Speed is a painting by J. M. W. Turner, dated 1844. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#4A2C18) up to sand (#C1B386), passing through brown, khaki, gray. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 21 to 73 in CIE Lab).
