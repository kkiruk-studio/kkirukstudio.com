About this palette

The Arnolfini Portrait is a painting by Jan van Eyck, dated 1434. It comes from Northern European art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from black (#191712) up to sand (#B8AD94), passing through dark umber, olive, rust, sienna. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 8 to 71 in CIE Lab).
