About this palette

The Jack Pine is a painting by Tom Thomson, dated 1917. It comes from North American art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#191E28) up to pale yellow (#F0DC96), passing through charcoal, moss green, ochre, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 11 to 88 in CIE Lab).
