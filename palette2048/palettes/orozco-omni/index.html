About this palette

Omnisciencia is a painting by José Clemente Orozco, dated 1925. It comes from Latin American art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1E1614) up to cream (#E6D2A5), passing through blue gray, rust, brown, ochre. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 8 to 85 in CIE Lab).
