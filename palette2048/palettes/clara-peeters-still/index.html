About this palette

Still Life with Cheeses is a painting by Clara Peeters, dated 1615. It comes from Northern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#1C0D06) up to beige (#E6CFAA), passing through sienna, brown, dusty blue, tan. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 5 to 84 in CIE Lab).
