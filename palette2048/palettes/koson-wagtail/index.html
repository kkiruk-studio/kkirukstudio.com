About this palette

Wagtail and Lotus is a painting by Ohara Koson, dated 1916. It comes from Japanese art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#192328) up to ivory (#E6E1CD), passing through steel blue, teal, dusty rose, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 13 to 89 in CIE Lab).
