About this palette

Boy in a Red Vest is a painting by Paul Cézanne, dated 1890. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#161B29) up to ivory (#EFEBD1), passing through charcoal, red, gray, silver gray. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 10 to 93 in CIE Lab).
