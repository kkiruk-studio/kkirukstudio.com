About this palette

Lady in the Moonlight is a painting by Raja Ravi Varma, dated 1889. It comes from South Asian art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from maroon (#3A202F) up to gray (#A89F93), passing through navy, charcoal, sienna. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 16 to 66 in CIE Lab).
