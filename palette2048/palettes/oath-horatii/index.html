About this palette

Oath of the Horatii is a painting by Jacques-Louis David, dated 1784. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#343230) up to silver gray (#D8D5CB), passing through red, slate gray, skin tone. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 21 to 85 in CIE Lab).
