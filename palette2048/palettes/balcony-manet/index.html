About this palette

The Balcony is a painting by Édouard Manet, dated 1869. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#141316) up to off-white (#F7F8F5), passing through deep blue, sea green, skin tone, silver gray. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 6 to 97 in CIE Lab).
