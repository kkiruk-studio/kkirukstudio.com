About this palette

The Fighting Temeraire is a painting by J. M. W. Turner, dated 1839. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#462719) up to beige (#D6CFB7), passing through brown, gray, terracotta, dusty blue. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 19 to 83 in CIE Lab).
