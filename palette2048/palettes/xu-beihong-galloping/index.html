About this palette

Galloping Horse is a painting by Xu Beihong, dated 1941. It comes from Chinese art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#32302B) up to silver gray (#B3B3A6), passing through charcoal, gray. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 20 to 73 in CIE Lab).
