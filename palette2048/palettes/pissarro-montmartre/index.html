About this palette

Boulevard Montmartre at Night is a painting by Camille Pissarro, dated 1897. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#0C0F2D) up to ivory (#EBEBD2), passing through burnt orange, dusty blue, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 6 to 92 in CIE Lab).
