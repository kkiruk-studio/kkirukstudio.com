About this palette

The Blue Boy is a painting by Thomas Gainsborough, dated 1770. It comes from Northern European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#0E1E26) up to slate gray (#71909E), passing through dark green, dark umber, blue gray. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, mostly cool (lightness 10 to 58 in CIE Lab).
