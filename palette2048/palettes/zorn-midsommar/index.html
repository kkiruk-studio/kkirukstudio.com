About this palette

Midsummer Dance is a painting by Anders Zorn, dated 1897. It comes from Northern European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#191E2D) up to cream (#EBD7AA), passing through blue gray, rust, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 11 to 87 in CIE Lab).
