About this palette

Tiger in a Tropical Storm is a painting by Henri Rousseau, dated 1891. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#142319) up to cream (#D2DCB4), passing through forest green, sienna, moss green, ochre. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 12 to 86 in CIE Lab).
