About this palette

Girl with Peaches is a painting by Valentin Serov, dated 1887. It comes from Eastern European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#2A2826) up to ivory (#E3E8DA), passing through terracotta, gold, sand, beige. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 16 to 91 in CIE Lab).
