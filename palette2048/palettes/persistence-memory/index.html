About this palette

The Persistence of Memory is a painting by Salvador Dalí, dated 1931. It comes from Southern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#16181A) up to powder blue (#B0D5C5), passing through brown, blue gray, gray, ochre. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 8 to 82 in CIE Lab).
