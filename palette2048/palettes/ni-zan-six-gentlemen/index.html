About this palette

Six Gentlemen is a painting by Ni Zan, dated 1345. It comes from Chinese art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from black (#1E1A14) up to cream (#EEE6C3), passing through dark umber, red, khaki. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 10 to 91 in CIE Lab).
