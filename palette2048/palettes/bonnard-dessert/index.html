About this palette

The Dessert is a painting by Pierre Bonnard, dated 1921. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1E191C) up to cream (#EBDCB4), passing through crimson, dusty blue, moss green, salmon. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 9 to 88 in CIE Lab).
