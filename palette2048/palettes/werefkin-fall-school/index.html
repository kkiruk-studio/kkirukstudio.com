About this palette

Fall (School) is a painting by Marianne von Werefkin, dated 1907. It comes from Eastern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#19161E) up to pale yellow (#EBCD82), passing through steel blue, rust, moss green, burnt orange. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 8 to 83 in CIE Lab).
