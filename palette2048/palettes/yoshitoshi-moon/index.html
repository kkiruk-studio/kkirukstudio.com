About this palette

One Hundred Aspects of the Moon is a painting by Tsukioka Yoshitoshi, dated 1892. It comes from Japanese art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from maroon (#490000) up to pale yellow (#FFE2A6), passing through brown, ochre, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 12 to 91 in CIE Lab).
