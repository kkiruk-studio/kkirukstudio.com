About this palette

The Elephant Celebes is a painting by Max Ernst, dated 1921. It comes from Northern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#0F1311) up to ivory (#E2DCCD), passing through charcoal, dark green, rust, brown. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 5 to 88 in CIE Lab).
