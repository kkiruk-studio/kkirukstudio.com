About this palette

Tamara in a Green Bugatti is a painting by Tamara de Lempicka, dated 1929. It comes from Eastern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#191E23) up to beige (#E1C8AF), passing through crimson, blue gray, teal, gray. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 11 to 82 in CIE Lab).
