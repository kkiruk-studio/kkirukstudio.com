About this palette

Portrait of a Singer is a painting by Nainsukh, dated 1750. It comes from South Asian art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#1C2019) up to cream (#EBE1C3), passing through steel blue, sea green, terracotta, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 12 to 90 in CIE Lab).
