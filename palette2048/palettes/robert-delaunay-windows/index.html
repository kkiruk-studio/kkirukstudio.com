About this palette

Simultaneous Windows on the City is a painting by Robert Delaunay, dated 1912. It comes from Northern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#2B213D) up to sand (#BEBA9B), passing through dark green, steel blue, sienna, khaki. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 15 to 75 in CIE Lab).
