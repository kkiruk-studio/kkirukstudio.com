About this palette

The Banjo Lesson is a painting by Henry Ossawa Tanner, dated 1893. It comes from North American art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1C1612) up to sand (#EBD2A0), passing through brown, blue gray, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 8 to 85 in CIE Lab).
