About this palette

General View of Mount Geumgang is a painting by Jeong Seon, dated 1734. It comes from Korean art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#181613) up to off-white (#D6DCDA), passing through brown, khaki, tan. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 7 to 87 in CIE Lab).
