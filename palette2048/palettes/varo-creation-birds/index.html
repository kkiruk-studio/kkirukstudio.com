About this palette

Creation of the Birds is a painting by Remedios Varo, dated 1957. It comes from Latin American art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from black (#1E1612) up to sand (#E1C896), passing through brown, crimson, blue gray, ochre. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 8 to 82 in CIE Lab).
