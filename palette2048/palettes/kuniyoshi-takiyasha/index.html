About this palette

Takiyasha the Witch and the Skeleton Spectre is a painting by Utagawa Kuniyoshi, dated 1844. It comes from Japanese art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#12161E) up to ivory (#E1DCCD), passing through charcoal, red, gray, khaki. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 7 to 88 in CIE Lab).
