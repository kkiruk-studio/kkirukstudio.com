About this palette

Composition II with Red, Blue and Yellow is a painting by Piet Mondrian, dated 1930. It comes from Northern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from maroon (#32121E) up to off-white (#E6E5DC), passing through steel blue, red, vermilion, yellow. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 11 to 91 in CIE Lab).
