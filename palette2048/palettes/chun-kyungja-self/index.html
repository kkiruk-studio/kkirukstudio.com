About this palette

Self-Portrait is a painting by Chun Kyung-ja, dated 1977. It comes from Korean art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from gold (#AC810A) up to emerald (#7FDEA2), passing through emerald. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly cool (lightness 57 to 81 in CIE Lab).
