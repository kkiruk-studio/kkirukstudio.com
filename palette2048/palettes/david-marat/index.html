About this palette

The Death of Marat is a painting by Jacques-Louis David, dated 1793. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#1D170A) up to pale yellow (#FFDE9F), passing through brown, skin tone, sand. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 8 to 90 in CIE Lab).
