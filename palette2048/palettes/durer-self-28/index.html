About this palette

Self-Portrait at 28 is a painting by Albrecht Dürer, dated 1500. It comes from Northern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#120E0E) up to skin tone (#E4BC9E), passing through dark umber, crimson, sienna, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 4 to 79 in CIE Lab).
