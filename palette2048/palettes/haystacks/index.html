About this palette

Haystacks (End of Summer) is a painting by Claude Monet, dated 1891. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dusty rose (#AF8B7E) up to off-white (#D0DEDE), passing through dusty blue, sage, pale yellow. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 61 to 87 in CIE Lab).
