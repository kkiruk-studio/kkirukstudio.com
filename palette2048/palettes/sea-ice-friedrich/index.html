About this palette

The Sea of Ice is a painting by Caspar David Friedrich, dated 1824. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from brown (#593523) up to silver gray (#CACFD8), passing through gray, sage, dusty blue, sky blue. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 26 to 83 in CIE Lab).
