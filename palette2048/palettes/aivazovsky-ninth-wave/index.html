About this palette

The Ninth Wave is a painting by Ivan Aivazovsky, dated 1850. It comes from Eastern European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#4F3C21) up to pale yellow (#EECF78), passing through teal, dusty rose, dusty blue, mustard. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 27 to 84 in CIE Lab).
