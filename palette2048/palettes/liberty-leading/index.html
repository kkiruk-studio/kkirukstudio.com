About this palette

Liberty Leading the People is a painting by Eugène Delacroix, dated 1830. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#0C0A17) up to ivory (#F4F0D7), passing through navy, crimson, slate gray, khaki. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 3 to 95 in CIE Lab).
