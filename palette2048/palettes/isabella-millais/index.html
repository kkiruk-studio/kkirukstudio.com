About this palette

Isabella is a painting by John Everett Millais, dated 1849. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#12100F) up to ivory (#F0E9D5), passing through red, burnt orange, gray, pink. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 5 to 92 in CIE Lab).
