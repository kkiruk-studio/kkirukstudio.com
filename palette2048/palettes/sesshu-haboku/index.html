About this palette

Haboku Landscape is a painting by Sesshū Tōyō, dated 1495. It comes from Japanese art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from black (#14120F) up to cream (#EBDAB4), passing through charcoal, red, khaki, sand. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 6 to 88 in CIE Lab).
