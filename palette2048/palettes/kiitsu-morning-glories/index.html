About this palette

Morning Glories is a painting by Suzuki Kiitsu, dated 1820. It comes from Japanese art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#000000) up to mustard (#F7C269), passing through charcoal, forest green, olive, moss green. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 0 to 82 in CIE Lab).
