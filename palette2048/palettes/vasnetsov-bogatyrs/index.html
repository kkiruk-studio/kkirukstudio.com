About this palette

Bogatyrs is a painting by Viktor Vasnetsov, dated 1898. It comes from Eastern European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1E1E23) up to beige (#E1DCC3), passing through brown, blue gray, gray, khaki. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 11 to 88 in CIE Lab).
