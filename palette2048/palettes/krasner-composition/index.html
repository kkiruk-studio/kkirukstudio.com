About this palette

Composition is a painting by Lee Krasner, dated 1949. It comes from North American art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from ultramarine (#3D4A8E) up to beige (#C7CDB3), passing through red, ochre, sea green, gold. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 34 to 81 in CIE Lab).
