About this palette

Among the Sierra Nevada is a painting by Albert Bierstadt, dated 1868. It comes from North American art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#18181E) up to ivory (#F8EED2), passing through dark green, gray, ochre, silver gray. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 8 to 94 in CIE Lab).
