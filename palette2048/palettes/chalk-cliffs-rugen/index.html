About this palette

Chalk Cliffs on Rügen is a painting by Caspar David Friedrich, dated 1818. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#1A1E14) up to off-white (#F6F3EE), passing through charcoal, crimson, tan, ivory. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 11 to 96 in CIE Lab).
