About this palette

Madame Récamier is a painting by Jacques-Louis David, dated 1800. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#26221E) up to ivory (#EBE0CE), passing through brown, khaki, gray. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 14 to 90 in CIE Lab).
