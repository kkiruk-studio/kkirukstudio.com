About this palette

The Block is a painting by Romare Bearden, dated 1971. It comes from North American art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from black (#20100B) up to sage (#C3D4A8), passing through red, sage. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 6 to 83 in CIE Lab).
