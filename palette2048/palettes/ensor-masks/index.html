About this palette

Self-Portrait with Masks is a painting by James Ensor, dated 1899. It comes from Northern European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1E191E) up to beige (#EBE1C8), passing through steel blue, teal, red, yellow. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 10 to 90 in CIE Lab).
