About this palette

Irises is a painting by Vincent van Gogh, dated 1889. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from deep blue (#232D6E) up to ivory (#EBE8DC), passing through cobalt blue, terracotta, sea green, gold. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 21 to 92 in CIE Lab).
