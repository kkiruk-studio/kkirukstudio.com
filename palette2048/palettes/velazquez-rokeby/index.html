About this palette

The Rokeby Venus is a painting by Diego Velázquez, dated 1651. It comes from Southern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#251D15) up to khaki (#B68B5F), passing through burgundy, dark umber, rust. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 11 to 61 in CIE Lab).
