About this palette

Dream Journey to the Peach Blossom Land is a painting by An Gyeon, dated 1447. It comes from Korean art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from olive (#8A7235) up to khaki (#C3B15C), passing through khaki, ochre. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 49 to 72 in CIE Lab).
