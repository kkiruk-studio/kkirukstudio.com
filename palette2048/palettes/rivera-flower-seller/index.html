About this palette

Flower Seller is a painting by Diego Rivera, dated 1942. It comes from Latin American art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from brown (#54321F) up to beige (#EFD8B4), passing through charcoal, crimson, sienna, ochre. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 25 to 87 in CIE Lab).
