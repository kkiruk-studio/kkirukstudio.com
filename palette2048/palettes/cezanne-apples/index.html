About this palette

Still Life with Apples is a painting by Paul Cézanne, dated 1894. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#283230) up to gold (#AC8B2B), passing through gray, terracotta. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 20 to 59 in CIE Lab).
