About this palette

The Monk by the Sea is a painting by Caspar David Friedrich, dated 1810. It comes from Northern European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#08141A) up to cream (#ECE1C4), passing through charcoal, dusty blue, khaki, powder blue. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 6 to 90 in CIE Lab).
