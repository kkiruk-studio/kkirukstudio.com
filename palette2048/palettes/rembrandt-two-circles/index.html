About this palette

Self-Portrait with Two Circles is a painting by Rembrandt van Rijn, dated 1665. It comes from Northern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#120C08) up to cream (#EDE0C4), passing through brown, sienna, tan. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 4 to 90 in CIE Lab).
