About this palette

The Lady of Shalott is a painting by John William Waterhouse, dated 1888. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1C1813) up to ivory (#E8DFCC), passing through dark green, brown, rust. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 9 to 89 in CIE Lab).
