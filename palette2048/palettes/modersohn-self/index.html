About this palette

Self-Portrait with Blue Irises is a painting by Paula Modersohn-Becker, dated 1905. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from dark green (#19281E) up to beige (#E6D7B4), passing through forest green, steel blue, sienna, skin tone. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 15 to 86 in CIE Lab).
