About this palette

Chaekgeori is a painting by Korean folk painting, dated 19th c.. It comes from Korean art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#191614) up to sand (#E1CDA0), passing through blue gray, crimson, forest green, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 8 to 83 in CIE Lab).
