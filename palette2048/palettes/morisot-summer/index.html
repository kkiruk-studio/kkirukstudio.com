About this palette

Summer's Day is a painting by Berthe Morisot, dated 1879. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from plum (#62536E) up to beige (#BEC7AC), passing through teal, gray, emerald, moss green. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 38 to 79 in CIE Lab).
