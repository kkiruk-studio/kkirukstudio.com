About this palette

The Tilled Field is a painting by Joan Miró, dated 1923. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1E1812) up to pale yellow (#F8DC94), passing through indigo, teal, burnt orange, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 9 to 89 in CIE Lab).
