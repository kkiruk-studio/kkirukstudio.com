About this palette

Music in the Tuileries is a painting by Édouard Manet, dated 1862. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#14190F) up to sage (#A8A884), passing through olive, forest green, steel blue, ochre. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 8 to 68 in CIE Lab).
