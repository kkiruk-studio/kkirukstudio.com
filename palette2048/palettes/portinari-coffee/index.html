About this palette

Coffee Plantation is a painting by Cândido Portinari, dated 1935. It comes from Latin American art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#391713) up to sand (#BEAB95), passing through rust, sienna. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 13 to 71 in CIE Lab).
