About this palette

Otani Oniji III is a painting by Tōshūsai Sharaku, dated 1794. It comes from Japanese art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#3D403A) up to beige (#F0D4B0), passing through gray, coral, tan. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 27 to 86 in CIE Lab).
