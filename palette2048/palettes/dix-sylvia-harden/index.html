About this palette

Portrait of the Journalist Sylvia von Harden is a painting by Otto Dix, dated 1926. It comes from Northern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from rust (#80372A) up to peach (#E3C0AC), passing through coral, salmon. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 33 to 80 in CIE Lab).
