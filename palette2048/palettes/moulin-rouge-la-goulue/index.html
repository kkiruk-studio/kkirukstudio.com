About this palette

Moulin Rouge: La Goulue is a painting by Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec, dated 1891. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#0E2432) up to yellow (#F0D34B), passing through rust, khaki, mustard, pale yellow. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 13 to 85 in CIE Lab).
