About this palette

Sarah Siddons as the Tragic Muse is a painting by Joshua Reynolds, dated 1784. It comes from Northern European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#100E0A) up to ivory (#F8F0DE), passing through dark umber, sienna, mustard, beige. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 4 to 95 in CIE Lab).
