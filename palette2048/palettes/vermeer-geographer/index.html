About this palette

The Geographer is a painting by Johannes Vermeer, dated 1669. It comes from Northern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#1A1711) up to pale yellow (#FFD896), passing through dark umber, brown, khaki, skin tone. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 8 to 88 in CIE Lab).
