About this palette

Wind God and Thunder God is a painting by Tawaraya Sōtatsu, dated c. 1630s. It comes from Japanese art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#231C14) up to ivory (#EBE6D7), passing through deep blue, forest green, red, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 11 to 91 in CIE Lab).
