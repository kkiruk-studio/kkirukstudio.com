About this palette

Annunciation is a painting by Simone Martini, dated 1333. It comes from Southern European art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from red (#B32609) up to silver gray (#D5D5DE), passing through orange, ochre, mustard. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 40 to 86 in CIE Lab).
