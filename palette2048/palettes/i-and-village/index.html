About this palette

I and the Village is a painting by Marc Chagall, dated 1911. It comes from Eastern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from maroon (#3E140C) up to beige (#E8DAC6), passing through ultramarine, rust, red, sea green. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 13 to 88 in CIE Lab).
