About this palette

The Battle of San Romano is a painting by Paolo Uccello, dated 1438. It comes from Southern European art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from black (#19191E) up to sand (#E1CD96), passing through steel blue, olive, red, sienna. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 9 to 83 in CIE Lab).
