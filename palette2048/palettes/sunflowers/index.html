About this palette

Sunflowers is a painting by Vincent van Gogh, dated 1888. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark green (#2A3915) up to mustard (#DEBA58), passing through rust, teal, ochre. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 22 to 77 in CIE Lab).
