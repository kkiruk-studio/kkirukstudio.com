About this palette

Flag is a painting by Jasper Johns, dated 1955. It comes from North American art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from deep blue (#032662) up to off-white (#FBE0ED), passing through red, crimson, pink. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 17 to 92 in CIE Lab).
