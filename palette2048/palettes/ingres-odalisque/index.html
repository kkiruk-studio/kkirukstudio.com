About this palette

La Grande Odalisque is a painting by Jean-Auguste-Dominique Ingres, dated 1814. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#090919) up to pale yellow (#FCD693), passing through navy, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 3 to 87 in CIE Lab).
