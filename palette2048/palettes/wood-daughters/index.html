About this palette

Daughters of Revolution is a painting by Grant Wood, dated 1932. It comes from North American art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#372105) up to peach (#F6BD7D), passing through brown, ochre, orange. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 15 to 80 in CIE Lab).
