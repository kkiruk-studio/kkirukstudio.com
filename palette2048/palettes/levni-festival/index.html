About this palette

Festival Miniature (Surname-i Hümayun) is a painting by Levni, dated 1720. It comes from Middle Eastern art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#1E1619) up to cream (#EBDCAF), passing through ultramarine, teal, red, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 8 to 88 in CIE Lab).
