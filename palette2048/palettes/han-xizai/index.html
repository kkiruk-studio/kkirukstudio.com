About this palette

Night Revels of Han Xizai is a painting by Gu Hongzhong, dated 10th c.. It comes from Chinese art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from maroon (#270803) up to sienna (#914511), passing through dark umber, brown, rust, red. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 6 to 38 in CIE Lab).
