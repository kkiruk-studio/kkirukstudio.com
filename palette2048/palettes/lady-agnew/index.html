About this palette

Lady Agnew of Lochnaw is a painting by John Singer Sargent, dated 1892. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#373033) up to ivory (#E7DACE), passing through brown, dusty blue, lavender, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 21 to 88 in CIE Lab).
