About this palette

Three Girls is a painting by Amrita Sher-Gil, dated 1935. It comes from South Asian art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from rust (#A53C25) up to beige (#DCD3B0), passing through steel blue, teal, moss green, mustard. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 40 to 84 in CIE Lab).
