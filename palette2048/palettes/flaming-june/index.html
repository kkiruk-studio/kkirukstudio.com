About this palette

Flaming June is a painting by Frederic Leighton, dated 1895. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#220906) up to ochre (#E7A85F), passing through maroon, blue gray, burnt orange, terracotta. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 5 to 73 in CIE Lab).
