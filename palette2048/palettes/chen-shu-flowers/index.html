About this palette

Flowers and Birds is a painting by Chen Shu, dated c. 1730. It comes from Chinese art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from olive (#827650) up to beige (#E0D3AF), passing through ochre, gray, tan, sand. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 50 to 85 in CIE Lab).
