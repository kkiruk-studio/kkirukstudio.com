About this palette

Black Iris is a painting by Georgia O'Keeffe, dated 1926. It comes from North American art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1C1D26) up to off-white (#EAEDEE), passing through blue gray, slate gray, gray, silver gray. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, mostly cool (lightness 11 to 94 in CIE Lab).
