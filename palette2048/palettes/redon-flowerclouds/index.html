About this palette

Flower Clouds is a painting by Odilon Redon, dated 1903. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#28324B) up to ivory (#EBE1D2), passing through dusty blue, sage, khaki, skin tone. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 21 to 90 in CIE Lab).
