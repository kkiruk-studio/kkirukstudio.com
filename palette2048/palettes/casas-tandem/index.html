About this palette

Ramon Casas and Pere Romeu on a Tandem is a painting by Ramón Casas, dated 1897. It comes from Southern European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1C1816) up to cream (#E6D7AF), passing through charcoal, rust, brown, ochre. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 9 to 86 in CIE Lab).
