About this palette

Self-Portrait in Tuxedo is a painting by Max Beckmann, dated 1927. It comes from Northern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#121216) up to off-white (#F2F0EC), passing through rust, blue gray, skin tone, silver gray. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 6 to 95 in CIE Lab).
