About this palette

Pilgrimage to Cythera is a painting by Antoine Watteau, dated 1717. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#302011) up to khaki (#958A5B), passing through dark green, rust, burnt orange. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 14 to 57 in CIE Lab).
