About this palette

The Gross Clinic is a painting by Thomas Eakins, dated 1875. It comes from North American art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#121110) up to dark umber (#423C36). It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 5 to 26 in CIE Lab).
