About this palette

Madame Moitessier is a painting by Jean-Auguste-Dominique Ingres, dated 1856. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#2D2B27) up to silver gray (#B9B6A4), passing through dusty rose, gray. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 18 to 74 in CIE Lab).
