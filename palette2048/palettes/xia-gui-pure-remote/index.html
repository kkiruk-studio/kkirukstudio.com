About this palette

Pure and Remote View of Streams and Mountains is a painting by Xia Gui, dated 1200. It comes from Chinese art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from black (#1A140F) up to ivory (#F7EAD2), passing through dark umber, rust, khaki, tan. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 7 to 93 in CIE Lab).
