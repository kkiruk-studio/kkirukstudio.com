About this palette

Primordial Chaos, No. 16 is a painting by Hilma af Klint, dated 1906. It comes from Northern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#141E37) up to ivory (#E6E1CD), passing through steel blue, sea green, ochre, yellow. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 12 to 89 in CIE Lab).
