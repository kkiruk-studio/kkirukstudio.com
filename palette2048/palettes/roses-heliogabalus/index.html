About this palette

The Roses of Heliogabalus is a painting by Lawrence Alma-Tadema, dated 1888. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#311910) up to silver gray (#EED1CF), passing through burgundy, crimson, dusty rose, powder blue. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 12 to 86 in CIE Lab).
