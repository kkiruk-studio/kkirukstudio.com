About this palette

The Garden of Love is a painting by Peter Paul Rubens, dated 1633. It comes from Western European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from midnight blue (#080D33) up to silver gray (#CBC3B7), passing through rust, ochre, steel blue, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 5 to 79 in CIE Lab).
