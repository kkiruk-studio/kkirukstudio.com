About this palette

Self-Portrait in a Straw Hat is a painting by Élisabeth Vigée Le Brun, dated 1782. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#322A26) up to beige (#E8DAC4), passing through blue gray, gray, dusty rose, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 18 to 88 in CIE Lab).
