About this palette

Charles I at the Hunt is a painting by Anthony van Dyck, dated 1635. It comes from Western European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#211D18) up to silver gray (#B0B8B8), passing through dark umber, brown, olive, gray. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 11 to 74 in CIE Lab).
