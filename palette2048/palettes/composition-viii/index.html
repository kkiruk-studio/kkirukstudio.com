About this palette

Composition VIII is a painting by Wassily Kandinsky, dated 1923. It comes from Eastern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from ultramarine (#2F5EA6) up to beige (#F0E1CA), passing through red, olive, tan, gold. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 40 to 90 in CIE Lab).
