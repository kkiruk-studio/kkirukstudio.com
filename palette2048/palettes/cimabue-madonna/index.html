About this palette

Santa Trinita Maestà is a painting by Cimabue, dated 1283. It comes from Southern European art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#2D211F) up to mustard (#D6A440), passing through sienna, ochre. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 14 to 70 in CIE Lab).
