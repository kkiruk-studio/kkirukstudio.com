About this palette

The Slave Ship is a painting by J.M.W. Turner, dated 1840. It comes from Northern European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#1C100A) up to cream (#FCEBC8), passing through red, orange, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 6 to 94 in CIE Lab).
