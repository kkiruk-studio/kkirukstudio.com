About this palette

The Child's Bath is a painting by Mary Cassatt, dated 1893. It comes from North American art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from dark umber (#2A201A) up to ivory (#ECE6DA), passing through teal, sienna, lavender, sky blue. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 13 to 91 in CIE Lab).
