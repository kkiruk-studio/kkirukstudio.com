About this palette

Irworobongdo (Sun, Moon and Five Peaks) is a painting by Korean court painting, dated 18th c.. It comes from Korean art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#142328) up to ivory (#E1DCCD), passing through ultramarine, blue gray, red, forest green. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 13 to 88 in CIE Lab).
