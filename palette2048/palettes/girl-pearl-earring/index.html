About this palette

Girl with a Pearl Earring is a painting by Johannes Vermeer, dated 1665. It comes from Northern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#1F1308) up to ivory (#EFE9D1), passing through navy, khaki, skin tone, beige. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 7 to 92 in CIE Lab).
