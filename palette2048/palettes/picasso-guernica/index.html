About this palette

Guernica is a painting by Pablo Picasso, dated 1937. It comes from Southern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from indigo (#232364) up to yellow (#F3CA49), passing through ochre, dusty rose, lavender, tan. It is a palette with moderate contrast with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 18 to 83 in CIE Lab).
