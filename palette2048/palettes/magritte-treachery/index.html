About this palette

The Treachery of Images is a painting by René Magritte, dated 1929. It comes from Northern European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#19150D) up to cream (#FEF3CC), passing through dark umber, brown, rust, skin tone. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 7 to 96 in CIE Lab).
