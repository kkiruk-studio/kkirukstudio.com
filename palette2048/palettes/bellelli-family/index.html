About this palette

The Bellelli Family is a painting by Edgar Degas, dated 1867. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#1F181F) up to sand (#B6AF92), passing through brown, gray, khaki, sage. It is a palette with moderate contrast with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 9 to 71 in CIE Lab).
