About this palette

Bharat Mata is a painting by Abanindranath Tagore, dated 1905. It comes from South Asian art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from sienna (#804511) up to mustard (#E4AD4C), passing through burnt orange, orange. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with rich, saturated tones, mostly warm (lightness 36 to 74 in CIE Lab).
