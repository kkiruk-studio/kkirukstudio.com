About this palette

A Bigger Splash is a painting by David Hockney, dated 1967. It comes from Northern European art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from terracotta (#B67855) up to powder blue (#C7E1DF), passing through steel blue, sky blue, pale yellow. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly cool (lightness 56 to 88 in CIE Lab).
