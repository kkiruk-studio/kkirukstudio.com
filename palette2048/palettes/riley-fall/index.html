About this palette

Fall is a painting by Bridget Riley, dated 1963. It comes from Southern European art of the postwar decades.

Its six colors run from black (#20190E) up to tan (#E2BE96), passing through dark green, olive, terracotta, skin tone. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 9 to 79 in CIE Lab).
