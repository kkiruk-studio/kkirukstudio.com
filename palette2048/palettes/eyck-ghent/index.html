About this palette

Ghent Altarpiece (detail) is a painting by Jan van Eyck, dated 1432. It comes from Northern European art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from black (#261214) up to peach (#FFD7B4), passing through maroon, brown, rust, terracotta. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 8 to 89 in CIE Lab).
