About this palette

Poplars is a painting by Claude Monet, dated 1891. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#2D2A3C) up to off-white (#E1EBF0), passing through dusty rose, sky blue, mustard, sage. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 18 to 92 in CIE Lab).
