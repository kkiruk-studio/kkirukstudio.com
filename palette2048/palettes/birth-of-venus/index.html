About this palette

The Birth of Venus is a painting by Sandro Botticelli, dated 1485. It comes from Southern European art of the centuries before 1500.

Its six colors run from charcoal (#374543) up to beige (#D5C3B5), passing through ochre, dusty rose, skin tone, powder blue. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 28 to 80 in CIE Lab).
