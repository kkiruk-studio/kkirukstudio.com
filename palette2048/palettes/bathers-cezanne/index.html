About this palette

The Bathers is a painting by Paul Cézanne, dated 1905. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from blue gray (#54686C) up to sand (#AFA28F), passing through sienna, gray, slate gray. It is a close-valued, low-contrast palette with muted tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 43 to 67 in CIE Lab).
