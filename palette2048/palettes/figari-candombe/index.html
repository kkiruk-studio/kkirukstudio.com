About this palette

Candombe is a painting by Pedro Figari, dated 1925. It comes from Latin American art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from black (#16161E) up to beige (#E6DCBE), passing through charcoal, crimson, teal, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 8 to 88 in CIE Lab).
