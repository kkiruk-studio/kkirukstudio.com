About this palette

Regatta at Cowes is a painting by Raoul Dufy, dated 1934. It comes from Western European art of the early 20th century.

Its six colors run from navy (#192846) up to off-white (#E6E6E1), passing through steel blue, coral, sea green, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, a mix of warm and cool (lightness 16 to 91 in CIE Lab).
