About this palette

In the Loge is a painting by Mary Cassatt, dated 1878. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#121209) up to khaki (#DAB774), passing through brown. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 5 to 76 in CIE Lab).
