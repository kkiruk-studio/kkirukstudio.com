About this palette

The Raft of the Medusa is a painting by Théodore Géricault, dated 1819. It comes from Western European art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#14110F) up to cream (#F4DCB2), passing through charcoal, rust, ochre, tan. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 5 to 89 in CIE Lab).
