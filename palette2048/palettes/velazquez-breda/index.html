About this palette

The Surrender of Breda is a painting by Diego Velázquez, dated 1635. It comes from Southern European art of the 16th and 17th centuries.

Its six colors run from black (#12100E) up to ivory (#EBE8DC), passing through brown, coral, dusty blue, mustard. It is a high-contrast palette with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 5 to 92 in CIE Lab).
