About this palette

Berthe Morisot with a Bouquet of Violets is a painting by Édouard Manet, dated 1872. It comes from Western European art of the second half of the 19th century.

Its six colors run from black (#100E0C) up to ivory (#DEDCD0), passing through navy, brown, silver gray, skin tone. It is a high-contrast palette with muted tones, mostly warm (lightness 4 to 88 in CIE Lab).
