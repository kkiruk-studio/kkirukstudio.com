About this palette

Pine Trees in Snow is a painting by Maruyama Ōkyo, dated 1786. It comes from Japanese art of the 18th and early 19th centuries.

Its six colors run from brown (#7A5D4D) up to beige (#ECD1BC), passing through dusty rose, skin tone. It is a palette with moderate contrast with balanced tones, mostly warm (lightness 42 to 86 in CIE Lab).
